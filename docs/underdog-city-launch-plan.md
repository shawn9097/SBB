## The decision (resolved)

**Primary discovery engine: YouTube. Release shape: full album drop as a
campaign. Spotify is the conversion layer, not the discovery layer.**

Why this and not the waterfall:
- Underdog City *is* the faceless concept act the YouTube playbook describes. The
  structural advantages (drillable lore, mask-as-thumbnail-face, bingeable
  concept catalog, "armchair detective" engagement) are exactly our strengths.
- The waterfall-singles model is optimized for Spotify's editorial machine,
  which an anonymous AI-assisted act with no label is least likely to win early.
- A concept album wants to be experienced as one world — that aligns with the
  lore and rewards session watch-time.

**The nuance that makes it work:** drop all the *music* at once, but stagger the
*video assets and promotion* over months so the release has a long tail instead
of a one-weekend spike.

### The funnel (memorize this)

```
YouTube Shorts (reach)  →  YouTube long-form / lore (familiarity + watch-time)
        →  Spotify + email/Discord (conversion + superfans)
```

Every single piece of content must point to the next step in that funnel.

## Do-now list (start regardless of timeline — these have long lead times)

- [ ] **Distribute through DistroKid** with delivery to YouTube Music (so Art
      Tracks + the Shorts sound picker get populated). *Learn-step 1.*
- [ ] **Claim the DistroKid Official Artist Channel (OAC)** — approval takes days
      to ~6 weeks, so start early. *Learn-step 2.*
- [ ] **Enroll every track in Content ID, set to MONETIZE (not block).** *Learn-step 3.*
- [ ] **Lock the artist name + exact spelling everywhere** ("Underdog City") and
      never change it mid-process — it breaks OAC/Topic linking.
- [ ] **Set up the channel shell:** themed banner, the masked-crown avatar,
      channel trailer, pinned "world" playlist. *Learn-step 4.*
- [ ] The website + email capture are already built — wire up the real ESP
      (Beehiiv/ConvertKit/Mailchimp) and a launch countdown date. *Learn-step 5.*

### Hard rules (don't violate these)

- **Music Shorts stay under 60 seconds.** Over 60s + a Content ID claim (even
  your own) = globally self-blocked since Dec 2025.
- **Never post Shorts with a TikTok watermark** — YouTube suppresses them.
- **Never buy streams, followers, or playlist placements** — catalog-wide
  termination risk.
- **Disclose AI involvement** through the distributor metadata as required.
- **Identical artist + track naming** across every platform.

## Phase 0 — Pre-launch (start 6–10 weeks before drop)

Goal: a small but rabid pre-release audience and a complete asset library, so
launch day has fuel instead of crickets. This is the PRESIDENT playbook scaled
down — lore + scarcity *before* a single note is public.

### 0.1 Infrastructure
- [ ] DistroKid distribution scheduled (set release date 4+ weeks out so editorial
      pitching is possible). *Learn-step 1.*
- [ ] OAC claim submitted. *Learn-step 2.*
- [ ] Content ID enrolled on all tracks. *Learn-step 3.*
- [ ] Channel branding live (banner/avatar/trailer/pinned playlist). *Learn-step 4.*
- [ ] ESP live with double opt-in + a welcome email; smart link / pre-save link
      created and circulating. *Learn-step 5 + 6.*
- [ ] Spotify for Artists + Apple Music for Artists access claimed. *Learn-step 7.*

### 0.2 Lore + scarcity (the pre-release engine)
- [ ] Cryptic teasers using the existing touchpoints (site, IG stories, Shorts).
- [ ] In-universe symbol/logo (the broken-crown/mask mark) on every asset.
- [ ] Countdown on the website.
- [ ] A Discord for the core community (fans tip ~200% more via Discord).
- [ ] ARG-style breadcrumbs — small, drillable lore that rewards diggers without
      being mandatory for casual viewers.
- [ ] A *named recurring series* people can subscribe FOR (e.g. "Transmissions"
      or "Lore Files") — gives a concrete reason to subscribe, not just view.

### 0.3 Pre-produce the full asset library (the big lift)
- [ ] **One flagship cinematic / "world reveal"** for the lead single. *Learn-step 8.*
- [ ] **A lyric video OR visualizer for every track.** *Learn-step 9.*
- [ ] **A gapless full-album stream video** (vary visuals per track). *Learn-step 9.*
- [ ] **30–50 batch-produced Shorts**, each with a first-2-second hook and a CTA. *Learn-step 10.*
- [ ] Cover art + Spotify Canvas (3–8s loops) per track. *Learn-step 11.*

## Phase 1 — Launch week

Goal: a coordinated multi-format event, not a single upload.

- [ ] Album live everywhere simultaneously (audio + same-day video assets — YT's
      own data says same-day videos drive higher first-2-week subs/views).
- [ ] Flagship video runs as a **Premiere** with a countdown + live chat; set a
      Live Redirect from a short pre-show stream into the Premiere. *Learn-step 12.*
- [ ] All lyric videos / visualizers uploaded the same day.
- [ ] Multiple Shorts dropped across launch day from different track moments.
- [ ] Streaming links pinned everywhere (descriptions above the fold, pinned
      comments, channel About, bio).
- [ ] Email blast to the pre-release list BEFORE public links go wide (give the
      list first access — that's the reward for subscribing early).
- [ ] Then sustain **2–3 Shorts/day for the 4–8 week launch window**, each with a
      hook + on-screen CTA + pinned comment to the relevant full video.

---

## Phase 2 — Sustain (months 2–6)

Goal: convert the launch spike into a compounding long tail.

- [ ] Drop to a sustainable **4–7 Shorts/week** at a **40 / 40 / 20 mix**:
      40% discovery Shorts, 40% lore/engagement long-form, 20% showcase (videos/visualizers).
- [ ] Give each album track its own dedicated **promo week** (its "moment").
- [ ] Release the deeper drillable lore content to reward the core fanbase.
- [ ] **Weekly analytics review** (see below) — redesign, re-hook, double down.
- [ ] At **500 subs (YPP Tier 1):** turn on memberships with exclusive lore tiers.
- [ ] Only amplify *proven* organic winners with small paid spend ($10–20/day),
      targeting fans of Sleep Token / Bad Omens / Bring Me The Horizon.

## The numbers that should change your behavior

| Signal | Threshold | What it means / do |
|---|---|---|
| Flagship CTR | < 3% | Thumbnail/title problem — fix packaging before anything else |
| Avg % viewed | < 50% | Hook too slow — move it earlier |
| Retention at 0:30 | sharp drop | First 30s is the leak — re-cut the open |
| Returning-viewer ratio | > 80% | Plateaued with new audiences — escalate Shorts variety/volume |
| Save rate (Spotify) | > 20% launch / ~4.5% floor | Healthy engagement signal |
| Skip rate (first 30s) | < 30% | Below this is healthy |

Realistic independent benchmark: **1K subs in 4–6 months** with daily Shorts
during the campaign; >4% CTR and >50% APV on the flagship. The S-curve is slow
to 1K, then compounds — patience is structural, not optional.

## The learn-steps index (the "teach me every step" track)

Each is a self-contained skill we'll walk through one at a time. They're roughly
in dependency order — earlier ones unblock later ones.

1. **Distribution** — DistroKid setup, metadata, AI disclosure, release scheduling
2. **Official Artist Channel** — claiming the OAC, Topic vs. personal channel
3. **Content ID** — enrolling tracks, monetize-not-block, eligibility rules
4. **Channel setup** — branding, trailer, playlists, the recurring series
5. **Email list** — ESP choice, double opt-in, welcome sequence (site is built)
6. **Smart links / pre-save** — the conversion link that ties platforms together
7. **Artist platform access** — Spotify for Artists, Apple Music for Artists
8. **The flagship cinematic** — concepting + producing the world-reveal video
9. **Lyric videos / visualizers / album stream** — the per-track asset pipeline
10. **Shorts production** — hooks, cuts, CTAs, batching, the sound picker
11. **Cover art + Spotify Canvas** — visual assets per track
12. **Premiere + live redirect** — running launch day as an event
13. **Analytics** — reading CTR/APV/retention/save-rate and acting on them
14. **Ongoing promotion** — the sustain cadence, memberships, paid amplification

We'll take these in order, one at a time, going deep on each. Nothing here is
assumed knowledge — each step gets explained from zero when we get to it.
