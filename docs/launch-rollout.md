## 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign name:** *The Gates Open* (rollout of *Throne at the Bottom*)

**One-sentence summary:** A three-week organic siege that turns a dark-fantasy world ("Underdog City") into a participation engine — fans become "tenants" who unlock locked transmissions, so every post is a piece of a story instead of an ad.

**Primary objective:** Convert cold short-form viewers into email subscribers ("key holders") and DSP followers before July 31.

**Secondary objectives:**
- Streams: bank Spotify **followers + album pre-saves** so the focus track lands in Release Radar *and* the album has day-one velocity.
- Social: grow the four channels (YT, TikTok, IG, FB) with a repeatable content engine.
- Proof of concept: validate that the world/lore hook drives engagement, not just the songs.

**The core strategic bet:** You are not marketing 14 songs. You are marketing *a place*. The lore ("We all rule down here," tenants, keys, redacted tracklist) is your unfair advantage — it makes people comment, theorize, and share in a way a normal single drop never will.

## 2. Target Audience

**Primary — "The Lore Kids" (Sleep Token / Bad Omens crowd):** aesthetic-first, concept-album listeners who follow a *world*. Screenshot lyrics, build theories, reward mystery. Live on TikTok, Instagram, r/SleepToken, r/BadOmens.

**Secondary — "The Mosh Crowd" (nu-metal revival):** want the chant hook and the riff. Live on YouTube, Facebook groups, r/numetal, r/metalcore.

**Where Underdog City wins:** dead center. Lead *aesthetic/lore* on TikTok+IG, lead *heaviness/hook* on YouTube+Facebook+Reddit. Almost everyone is at "never heard of you," so the funnel is: **short-form hook → profile/world → email key → follower → streamer.**

## 8. Risks & Mitigations
- **Zero content, 3 weeks** → batch sprint Jul 9–11; 2 weeks of clips up front.
- **"Stupid Little Bitch" flags the brand** → keep it *off* short-form/ads; album/DSP only. Lead with Villain, Down Here, Who TF.
- **Reddit backlash/bans** → never lead with a link; one sub at a time; contribute first; a fresh AI-music account is fragile.
- **Album drops to silence** → the pre-save/follow link + a follower-first CTA on every post prevent this. Do not skip Phase 0.
- **Solo burnout** → the batch system + sustain plan. (See "right-sizing" note below.)

## 9. Reality check — right-size it
This plan is the *map*, not a daily obligation. For a solo person who's still building the content muscle: the real minimum is **one post a day with one clear CTA.** Everything else is bonus. Nailing a simple daily post beats freezing on an elaborate one. Pick ONE CTA per post (pre-save *or* email — never both in one breath) to avoid splitting attention.
