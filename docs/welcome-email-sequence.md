# Underdog City — Welcome Email Sequence

Three emails, set up as a **welcome automation** in Beehiiv (fires after a new
subscriber confirms). Keep them short and voice-y. Swap `[YouTube]` / `[TikTok]`
for your real links. Sending domain should be authenticated (theunderdogcity.com)
so these land in inboxes.

---

## Email 1 — send immediately (on confirm)

**Subject:** Welcome to Underdog City, tenant.
**Preview:** Your key works. Here's what you just walked into.

**Body:**

You're in.

The world spends a lot of time deciding who's worth something. You just walked
into the one place that stopped asking.

Down here, the broken things hold the most power. Everything they threw away —
that's what we built a throne out of. You're a tenant now, which means you're one
of the first people on Earth who even knows Underdog City exists.

Here's what that gets you:

- **The music first.** When the first anthem drops, you hear it before the world does.
- **The world.** There's a whole story down here, and you'll get pieces of it nobody else does.
- **No spam, no filler.** Just transmissions from the bottom.

The debut album — **Throne at the Bottom** — arrives **July 31.** Fourteen tracks.
Turn that shit up loud.

Welcome home.
— Underdog City

*P.S. Come say what's up: [YouTube] · [TikTok]. The face stays hidden. The music doesn't.*

## Email 2 — send ~2 days later

**Subject:** They threw us away. So we built a throne.
**Preview:** A short transmission from the bottom of the city.

**Body:**

There's a world above this one. They call it the Halo — all that clean light,
deciding who's worth saving and who gets dropped.

We're the dropped.

Here's what they never understood: when you hit the bottom hard enough, something
changes. The break lets the gold in. Down here, your damage stops being the thing
that ruined you and starts being the thing that makes you dangerous.

That's Underdog City. Everyone the world discarded, building a kingdom out of
everything that was thrown away.

We all rule down here. You're part of it now.

More soon.
— Underdog City

You're on this list because you get it first — before it hits streaming, before

When the day comes, the music lands in your inbox before it goes public anywhere

Until then, the transmissions are starting to leak out — the hooks, the fragments,
the first look at the face nobody's supposed to see. It's all going out here:

**[YouTube] · [TikTok]**

Get loud with us.

---

## Setup notes
- These are the **welcome automation**, separate from Beehiiv's double opt-in
  confirmation email (that one fires first, before Email 1).
- Turn the automation **ON** — a welcome email that isn't activated never sends.
- Authenticate the sending domain (theunderdogcity.com) in Beehiiv → Domains so
  these reach the inbox instead of spam.
- On launch day (July 31), send a **one-off broadcast** to the whole list with the
  smart link, *before* you post the links publicly.
